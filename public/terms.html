You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. By registering you confirm that you are.

2. Your account

Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Please use one account per person.

3. Fair play

  • No cheating: no calculators or outside help where a match calls for your own thinking, no bots, no scripts, no sharing answers.
  • No harassment, hate or threats, including in usernames, display names and profile pictures.
  • No impersonating other players or staff, and no trying to break or overload the service.

4. Moderation

Moderators and administrators can suspend or ban accounts that break these rules. A suspension lasts for a set time; a ban is permanent unless lifted. When this happens you will see the reason the next time you try to sign in. Ratings and results from unfair matches may be adjusted or removed.

5. Your content

Your username, display name and profile picture are visible to other players. You must have the right to use any picture you upload. We may remove content that breaks these rules.
