Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-24
Draft. This is a plain-language starting point written for the current features of Minduel. Have it reviewed by a lawyer, and fill in the bracketed parts, before you launch publicly.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email address, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form).
  • Profile: display name and, if you choose one, a profile picture. Pictures are cropped and re-encoded in your browser, which removes camera data such as location.
  • Play data: your rating, match results and answers, once matches exist.
  • Consent record: the date and version of the Terms you accepted, and that you confirmed you are at least 13.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

Who can see what

Other players can see your username, display name, profile picture, rating, any staff badge, and anything you choose to add to your profile (bio, location, social links). Friends can also see when you are online, and your friends can send you private messages. Messages are stored on our servers so the conversation can be shown to both of you. Your email address is never shown to other players. Administrators can see account emails in order to run the service, and moderators can see moderation history for accounts they act on.

Why we use it

To run your account, match you with opponents, keep the game fair, secure the service, and send password-reset emails you request.

On your device

Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in. Logging out removes it. Changing your password signs out all other devices.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a managed MongoDB database and served by our hosting provider [your hosting region / providers].

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. To ask for your data to be deleted, contact [your contact email].

Children

Minduel is not for children under 13. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 13 we will remove it.
